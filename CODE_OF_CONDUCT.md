# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, responsibility for mistakes, focus on what is best for the community, and support for other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, trolling, personal attacks, publishing private information without permission, and conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject contributions and communication that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the repository owner through an established trusted channel. Do not publish personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
